production activation. Extract shared command policy into acgm_policy.py; keep

adding a YAML dependency. Require successful matching tool results, same-host

waived by prose fields. No opaque wrapper receives transport privileges.

fields, enlarging the window, or copying a second policy into the Codex adapter.
